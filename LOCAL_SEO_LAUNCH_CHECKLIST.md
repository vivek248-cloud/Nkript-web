# Nkript local SEO launch checklist

- Update the canonical domain and structured data to the actual live domain.
- Add verified business phone, email, address or service area. Do not publish fake details.
- Upload authentic completed projects, screenshots and accurate project descriptions.
- Create genuinely useful service pages for website development, e-commerce, billing software, redesign and digital marketing. Avoid near-identical keyword pages.
- Add sitemap.xml and robots.txt and submit the sitemap in Google Search Console.
- Set up Google Business Profile if eligible and keep business details consistent.
- Ask real customers for honest reviews; never fabricate testimonials, client counts or ranking claims.
- Monitor impressions and enquiries with Search Console and analytics.
- Keywords such as “best web developer in Trichy”, “website builders near me” and “best web design company in Trichy” are targets, not guaranteed rankings. Search engines decide rankings; SEO cannot promise the top position.
